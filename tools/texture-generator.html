Snowcube texture atlases

The two atlases work differently. cube-atlas.png is full colour and defines the cube's appearance outright. tile-atlas.png is a greyscale detail map that three.js multiplies by material.color, because tiles recolour at runtime — keep its pixels light and completely hue-free or you will tint the whole palette. Edit the drawing functions below, reload, and download. Save the files into public/textures/.

cube-atlas.png — 3072 × 2048, 3 × 2 cells of 1024px

This one is full colour. It carries the cube's hue itself, so the renderer drops the material tint to white once it loads. Cells run left-to-right then top-to-bottom and map onto BoxGeometry's material slots. Keep each cell in its palette colour — the rules match on the face colour, so recolouring a cell desyncs what you see from what scores.

    tile-atlas.png — 1024 × 1024, 3 × 3 cells

    This one stays greyscale. Tiles change colour at runtime, so the board tint has to come from material.color. Cells 0–5 are the six palette colours (Ruby, Amber, Citrine, Jade, Azure, Violet), cell 6 is the neutral snow tile, cells 7–8 are spare. Each colour gets its own pattern so a board of mixed tiles reads clearly.
