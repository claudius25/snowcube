Snowcube texture atlases

Both atlases are full colour and define their surfaces outright — the renderer drops material.color to white once they load. Every cell is only ever shown for one palette colour, so keep each cell in its own hue: the rules match on colour, and recolouring a cell desyncs what you see from what scores. Detail, pattern and shading are yours to change. Edit the drawing functions below, reload, and download. Save the files into public/textures/.

cube-atlas.png — 3072 × 2048, 3 × 2 cells of 1024px

This one is full colour. It carries the cube's hue itself, so the renderer drops the material tint to white once it loads. Cells run left-to-right then top-to-bottom and map onto BoxGeometry's material slots. Keep each cell in its palette colour — the rules match on the face colour, so recolouring a cell desyncs what you see from what scores.

    tile-atlas.png — 3072 × 3072, 3 × 3 cells of 1024px

    Cells 0–5 are the six palette colours, cell 6 is the neutral snow tile a cleared tile returns to, and cells 7–8 are spare. Each colour gets its own pattern so a board of mixed tiles still reads clearly when colours sit side by side.
